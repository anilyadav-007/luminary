Who We Are

Luminary was founded in 2018 by a small team of designers, strategists, and storytellers who believed the best creative work comes from deep listening — not just clever execution.

Today we partner with founders, brands, and institutions across the world to help them find their voice and make it impossible to ignore. We're small by design, obsessive by nature.

Every project begins with one question: what do you want people to feel? The answer shapes everything we make.

The things we
believe in.

01 Honesty

We tell clients what they need to hear, not what they want. Good creative work is a partnership, and that starts with trust.

02 Craft

Details matter enormously. The curve of a letterform, the weight of a pause — we sweat the small stuff because small things compound.

03 Curiosity

Every brief is a chance to learn something new. We approach each project like a student — asking questions, testing assumptions, staying open.

04 Restraint

We believe in removing, not adding. The best solution is usually the simplest one — and getting there takes discipline.
